FEES AND CHARGES
* No fee is collected for inclusion, deletion, or change of name/address in the Ration Card (FAQ Q15).
* Fee for new ration card issuance: NOT SPECIFIED IN SOURCES.
STATUTORY TIMELINES
* Issue of a new Ration Card: 30 days (Citizen Charter).
* Addition or Deletion of family member: 30 days (Citizen Charter).
* Change of address or FPS within the same circle: 30 days (Citizen Charter).
* Surrender of Food Card: Same day (Citizen Charter).
* Note: Timelines are strictly subject to verification and the availability of beneficiary vacancies under the 72.78 lakh G.O.I. limit (FAQ Q7).
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
* For New Card: Aadhaar Card of all family members, passport-size photo of Head of Family (HoF), latest electricity bill, and residence proof if different from HoF’s Aadhaar address (FAQ Q8).
* For Address Change: Copy of Ration Card, proof of new address, copy of electricity bill (FAQ Q13).
* For Inclusion/Deletion: Copy of old NFS Ration Card, reasons for change, and copy of Aadhaar/EID for new inclusions (FAQ Q14).
* For Servant: Authorization letter and address proof (FAQ Q32).

GROUNDS FOR REFUSAL AND REVOCATION
* Rejection if the maximum beneficiary limit of 72.78 lakh fixed by the Government of India has been exhausted (FAQ Q4 & Q7).
* Rejection if the application fails document or physical verification by the FSO (FAQ Q7).
* Explicit administrative cancellation grounds: NOT SPECIFIED IN SOURCES.

OFFICER DESIGNATIONS
* Competent Authority for Approval/Modifications: Food & Supply Officer (FSO) (Citizen Charter).
* Appellate Authority: District Grievance Redressal Officer (Citizen Charter).
VARIATION RULES
* Head of Family (HoF): Must be the eldest female member (18 years & above). If none exists, the elder male member becomes HoF (FAQ Q2 & Q3).
* Eligibility (Income): Households having an annual income of less than Rs. 1 Lakh (FAQ Q1).
* Eligibility (Vulnerable Groups): Residents of slums/resettlement colonies, shelterless, transgenders, disabled persons, single women/widows, children without protection, and unskilled occupationally vulnerable groups (rag-pickers, casual labor, rickshaw drivers) (FAQ Q1).
