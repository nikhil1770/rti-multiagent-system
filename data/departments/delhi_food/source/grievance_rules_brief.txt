FEES AND CHARGES
* Filing a grievance or appeal: NOT SPECIFIED IN SOURCES.
* Deposit requirement: Any licensee appealing a cancellation/termination order must deposit the penalty and liability amount exceeding their security deposit before filing the appeal (Gazette 2026 Clause 6(B)).
STATUTORY TIMELINES
* FPS Allotment Appeals: Must be filed before the Commissioner within 30 days of communication of the decision. Disposed of by a reasoned order within 45 days (Gazette 2026 Clause 6(A)).
* FPS Cancellation Appeals: Must be filed within 30 days from the date of receipt of the order. May be entertained after 30 days if sufficient cause is shown (Gazette 2026 Clause 6(B)).
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
* Appeals regarding FPS license cancellation or termination must be made "in writing" (Gazette 2026 Clause 6(B), 6(G)).

OFFICER DESIGNATIONS
* Appellate Authority for Ration Card services: District Grievance Redressal Officer / Additional District Magistrates (Citizen Charter).
* First level PDS complaints: Concerned Circle FSO or Vigilance Committee (Citizen Charter).
* Harassment by officials: Spl./Addl./Joint Commissioner or Commissioner, Food & Supplies (Citizen Charter).
* FPS Allotment Appeals: Commissioner (Gazette 2026 Clause 6(A)).
* FPS Cancellation Appeals: Commissioner or Authorised Officer (not below Deputy Commissioner) (Gazette 2026 Clause 6(B)).
* Subsequent Appeals: Financial Commissioner, Govt. of NCT of Delhi (Gazette 2026 Clause 6(G)).
* Revisionary Powers: Lieutenant Governor (suo-moto) (Gazette 2026 Clause 6(H)).
VARIATION RULES
* PDS Outlet Complaint Categories: Measurement, quality, excess charging, refusal to issue commodities, black marketing, unfair/restrictive trade practices, defect in goods, deficiency in services, hazardous goods (Citizen Charter Page 7 & 9).
