FEES AND CHARGES
* Application fee: ₹1,000 for Regular license, ₹500 for Temporary license (Gazette 2026 Clause 3(I)).
* One-time license fee: ₹10,000 (Gazette 2026 Clause 3(K)(c)).
* Duplicate license fee: ₹500 (Gazette 2026 Clause 3(K)(d)).
* Transition fee for existing licensees: ₹5,000 (Gazette 2026 Clause 5).
* Performance Guarantee: ₹50,000 (up to 500 cards); ₹75,000 (501-750 cards); ₹1,00,000 (more than 750 cards) (Gazette 2026 Clause 3(K)).
STATUTORY TIMELINES
* License applications must be submitted within 2 weeks of advertisement (Gazette 2026 Clause 3(H)).
* Make good stock deficit (Minor Irregularity): within 1 week (Gazette 2026 Clause 4(B)).
* Remittance of forfeited penalty amounts: within 15 calendar days (Gazette 2026 Clause 4).
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
* Documents as specified in Annexure-I for online portal submission (Gazette 2026 Clause 3(H)).
MANDATORY NOCS / APPROVALS
* NOT SPECIFIED IN SOURCES.
GROUNDS FOR REFUSAL AND REVOCATION
* Disqualifications for license: Applicant is of unsound mind, previous license cancelled for malpractice, already holds a restricted license, conviction or charges under ECA 1955/Bhartiya Nyaya Sanhita/moral turpitude, or furnished false information (Gazette 2026 Clause 3(F)).
* Cancellation/Suspension (Major Irregularity): Stock variation of 50-100 kg SFAs leads to ₹10,000 forfeit, immediate suspension, and quasi-judicial proceedings (Gazette 2026 Clause 4(C)).
* Cancellation/Suspension (Serious Irregularity): Stock variation >100 kg leads to entire security forfeit, immediate suspension, FIR under ECA Sec 7/10, and quasi-judicial proceedings (Gazette 2026 Clause 4(D)).
* Cancellation/Forfeit up to ₹25,000 for operational violations: Failure to display info/samples, tampering with e-PoS, no fire safety, discourteous behavior, unhygienic conditions (Gazette 2026 Clause 4(E)).
STANDARD CONDITIONS IMPOSED
* Timings: 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. and 3:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. (Citizen Charter).
* Weekly off on Monday and closed on Jan 26, Aug 15, and Oct 2 (Gazette 2026 Clause 10).
* Must operate via Department-approved e-PoS system, ensure real-time reporting, and display statutory info (Gazette 2026 Clause 10).
* Entitlement Distribution: Priority Households (4Kg wheat @ Rs 2.00, 1Kg rice @ Rs 3.00); AAY (25kg wheat @ Rs 2.00, 10kg rice @ Rs 3.00) (Citizen Charter).
OFFICER DESIGNATIONS
* Authorized Officer or Commissioner (for suspension, quasi-judicial proceedings, and cancellations) (Gazette 2026 Clause 4).
VARIATION RULES
* Eligibility: Age 21-60 years (relaxable by 5 years for women/ex-servicemen), Class XII pass, resident of same revenue district (Gazette 2026 Clause 3(E)).
* Limit on Licenses: Individuals get one license; SHGs/Societies/NGOs may get up to 5 per revenue district (Gazette 2026 Clause 3(D)).
