FEES AND CHARGES
Regularisation requires paying requisite fees and charges (UBBL Clause 2.27).
Compounding charges for FAR/coverage deviations (UBBL Annexure IV (B)):
Up to 0.5%: Same charges as for permissible additional FAR.
Above 0.5% to 2.5%: 5 times the charges as for permissible additional FAR.
Above 2.5% to 5%: 10 times the charges as for permissible additional FAR.
Explicit Penalty Amounts: NOT SPECIFIED IN SOURCES.
STATUTORY TIMELINES
Alteration/Stoppage Show Cause Notice: Must provide a period of not less than seven days to make alterations or show cause (DMC Act Sec 345(1)).
Demolition of Unfit Building: Must be vacated within a period not less than 30 days from the date of the order, and demolished within six weeks after the expiration of that period (DMC Act Sec 368(3)).
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
Form D-4 for regularisation (UBBL Clause 2.27).
MANDATORY NOCS / APPROVALS
Approval of the Standing Committee is required to order the pulling down of a building within the regular line of a street (DMC Act Sec 307(2)).
GROUNDS FOR ENFORCEMENT (DEMOLITION/SEALING/NOTICES)
Building within the regular line of a public street (DMC Act Sec 307).
Laying out a street contrary to Standing Committee orders (DMC Act Sec 314).
Erection contrary to sanction, conditions, or bye-laws (DMC Act Sec 345).
Building in ruinous condition, likely to fall, or dangerous (DMC Act Sec 348).
Building unfit for human habitation and not capable of being rendered fit at a reasonable expense (DMC Act Sec 368).
STANDARD CONDITIONS IMPOSED
Sealing orders can be made before or after making an order of demolition or stoppage of erection. No person shall remove the seal except under an order by the Commissioner, Appellate Tribunal, or Administrator (DMC Act Sec 345A).
OFFICER DESIGNATIONS
Commissioner (initiates notices, sealing, demolition).
Standing Committee.
Appellate Tribunal.
Administrator.
VARIATION RULES
Compounding Limits: Authority can compound deviations from limits of coverage/FAR to the extent of 5% of permissible coverage and FAR, subject to a maximum of 13.5 sq.m. For group housing and public/semi-public facilities, 5% FAR beyond permissible can be compounded (UBBL Annexure IV (B) & Clause 3(12)).
