FEES AND CHARGES
Building Permit fee for Completion: Calculated at the rate of Rs. 10/- per sq.m of covered area (including basement and stilt floor) (UBBL Clause 1.a.i).
Application for completion certificate fee: Rs. 10/- per sq.m of covered area (UBBL Clause 2.1.1.b).
Inspection of completion work up to plinth level fee: Rs. 500/- (UBBL Clause 2.1.1.a).
Pre-occupancy charges: Rs. 25,000/- per dwelling unit for group housing; Rs. 2000/- per sq.m for Commercial buildings; Rs. 500/- per sq.m for Institutional/Govt./Others (UBBL Clause 2.1.1.c).
STATUTORY TIMELINES
Inspection and Communication: The Authority must inspect the work and communicate the sanction, refusal, or objection within 60 days from the date of receipt of the notice of completion (Handbook Clause 10.I).
Deemed Occupancy: NOT SPECIFIED IN SOURCES.

Structural drawings as executed, signed by owner, Architect, and Structural Engineer.
Copy of Lease-deed.
Valid extension of time (if applicable) up to the date of applying.
Revalidation of plan up to the date of application.
Valid Certificates for Architect, Engineer, and Qualification Certificate for Structural Engineer.
Certificate regarding deviations from Architect/Engineer.
One set of coloured building photographs (8x10).
3 sets (one cloth mounted) of Completion Plans (Layout, Building, Sections & Elevations) with actual area calculations.
Superimposed Layout and Building Plans (where applicable).
Sewer connection permission from DDA (wherever applicable).

GROUNDS FOR REFUSAL AND REVOCATION
Rejection: The Authority will communicate in case of rejection, giving full reasons and objections raised (UBBL Clause 2.26.3.b).
Revocation: The Completion/Occupancy Certificate shall be revoked, and action taken as per law, if it is found that it was obtained on misrepresentation (UBBL Clause 2.29.1.b).
